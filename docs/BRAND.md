# Wichita Life brand standards (reference)

Summary of the Wichita Life Brand Standards Manual v1.0 (2022). Reference for future design work; the app
doesn't follow all of it yet, and nothing here should be changed in the app without asking first.

## Logo
- Versions: primary lock-up (WL mark above "WICHITA LIFE"), logomark alone, horizontal (mark left, two-line
  wordmark right). Each comes in color, line (navy outline on white) and negative (white on navy).
- Prefer the primary lock-up. The logomark can stand alone; the logotype (wordmark) never appears alone.
- Clear space around the logo = the average height of the wordmark letters (minimum); give it more when possible.
- Don't rotate, rearrange, outline, distort, recolor, or add strokes to the logo.
- On navy or blue backgrounds the wordmark is white. On red backgrounds use the all-white version. On secondary
  colors the logo is white on dark or navy on light. Aim for the highest contrast.

## Type
- Brand typeface: **Montserrat** (Light, Regular, Bold, Black). Bold/Black for headings and subheadings,
  Light/Regular for body text. Line spacing about 5pt more than the default.
- **Caveat Brush** is for newsletter headers only, always lowercase/sentence case (all caps is reserved for the logotype).

## Graphic elements
- Hand-drawn icons in red with a blue offset shadow and navy outline, often on a Retro Yellow circle:
  camera (photography), handshake + heart (community), speaker (music), envelope (newsletter), mic (podcast),
  calendar, art supplies (artists), Keeper of the Plains (locations); supporting star, heart, music note,
  hot-air balloon.
- Hand-drawn Wichita skyline illustration (red/blue/yellow) with individual landmarks that can be used for
  events near those places.
- Newsletter section headers: Retro Yellow bars with a navy outline and blue shadow, Caveat Brush label and an
  icon (Weather, Events, Podcast, Local, Community, News, Rewards).
- Section label bars in the manual: Strawberry bar, navy outline, blue shadow.
