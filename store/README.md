# Store files

- `screenshots/iphone/`: App Store screenshots, 1206 × 2622, for the required "iPhone with Dynamic Island (medium display)" slot. Upload in order, 1 to 8. (Apple stopped taking 1320 × 2868 in that slot in late 2026; the optional iPhone Duo slot uses 1398 × 2034 and 2007 × 2853.)
- `screenshots/android/`: Google Play phone screenshots, 1080 × 1920 (Google rejects anything taller than 2:1). Same order.
- `google-play/`: the 512 × 512 store icon and the 1024 × 500 feature graphic Google Play requires.

Store text (description, keywords, reviewer notes) is in `docs/STORE_LISTING.md`; the App Privacy and
Age Rating answers are in `APP_STORE_FORMS.md`.
